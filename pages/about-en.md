---
title: "About the hub"
---

The Swedish Influenza Forecast Hub is a research pilot at the Department of Mathematical Sciences, Chalmers University of Technology.

Its purpose is to make independent influenza forecasts comparable, evaluate them openly and investigate whether a probabilistic ensemble provides more robust decision support than individual models.

## Data source

Observed data are retrieved from the Public Health Agency of Sweden's Folkhälsodata database. The retrospective view uses the frozen final version for the 2025/26 season.

## Openness

The formats, validation tools, test models, ensemble code and evaluation results are available in the [hub's GitHub repository](https://github.com/philipgerlee/swedish_forecasting_hub).
